It was written by the demo author solely to show how a compliance-checking system would work. Do not treat any rule here as reflecting real PW practices.**

## Scope
Applies to: Student/parent counselling calls handled by the Physics Wallah demo counselling team (fictional).
Version: v1 | Effective: 2026-10-01 | Owner: Demo QA team

## Rules

### R-01 · Truthful Product Claims · Severity: **critical**
**Description:** Counsellors must not make false or unverifiable claims about course outcomes, faculty credentials, rank results, or selection guarantees.

**Example violation:** "Hamare course se 100% selection hoti hai JEE mein" (claiming guaranteed selection).

**Compliant phrasing:** "Hamare students ne kaafi achhe results diye hain. Main aapko specific data de sakta hoon lekin guarantee dena mere liye possible nahi hai."

---

### R-02 · No Urgency / Pressure Tactics · Severity: **critical**
**Description:** Counsellors must not create artificial urgency through false scarcity ("last 2 seats"), countdown pressure, or threats of price increase that are not verifiably scheduled.

**Example violation:** "Sirf 2 seats bachi hain, aaj hi enroll karo warna price double ho jaayegi."

**Compliant phrasing:** "Batch ke seats limited hoti hain, main actual availability check karke bata sakta hoon."

---

### R-03 · Scholarship Accuracy · Severity: **critical**
**Description:** Any scholarship percentage or discount mentioned must match the current official offer. Counsellors must not invent or inflate scholarship amounts.

**Example violation:** "Aapko 80% scholarship milegi" (when actual max is 50%).

**Compliant phrasing:** "Current scholarship structure ke hisaab se aapko [verified percentage] tak mil sakti hai, jo eligibility test ke baad decide hogi."

---

### R-04 · Competitor Disparagement · Severity: **major**
**Description:** Counsellors must not make unverifiable negative claims about competitors (Allen, Aakash, Unacademy, etc.). Comparative statements must be factual and verifiable.

**Example violation:** "Allen mein faculty bahut kharab hai, wahan se koi nahi nikalta."

**Compliant phrasing:** "Alag-alag platforms ke apne strengths hote hain. Hamare approach ke baare mein main aapko batata hoon."

---

### R-05 · Disclosure of Course Limitations · Severity: **major**
**Description:** Counsellors must proactively mention material limitations that affect the student's decision: no offline classes if student prefers offline, subject coverage gaps, schedule conflicts.

**Example violation:** Selling an online-only batch to a student who said they need offline coaching without disclosing this.

**Compliant phrasing:** "Yeh batch fully online hai. Aapne offline prefer kiya tha — shall we check if we have an offline option?"

---

### R-06 · Parent Consent for Minors · Severity: **major**
**Description:** If the student is in Class 11 or below, the counsellor must confirm that the parent/guardian is aware and agrees to the purchase before processing or strongly recommending enrollment.

**Example violation:** Proceeding with a Class 10 student's enrollment without any mention of parental consent.

**Compliant phrasing:** "Aapke parents ko bhi ek baar bata dena chahiye — kya woh available hain ya main unhe call kar sakta hoon?"

---

### R-07 · EMI Terms Must Be Accurate · Severity: **major**
**Description:** If an EMI option is mentioned, the counsellor must state the correct number of instalments, the processing fee (if any), and the total amount. Rounding or guessing is not permitted.

**Example violation:** "3 installments mein ho jaayega, koi extra charge nahi" (when a processing fee applies).

**Compliant phrasing:** "3 EMIs hain — [amount each]. Ek ₹X processing fee bhi lagti hai. Total [verified amount] hota hai."

---

### R-08 · No Unauthorized Discounts · Severity: **major**
**Description:** Counsellors must not offer discounts or fee waivers beyond their authorised limit without written approval. Offering an unauthorised discount to close a sale is prohibited.

**Example violation:** "Main aapke liye ₹2000 extra discount de deta hoon personally" (outside policy).

**Compliant phrasing:** "Main current offer check karta hoon — agar koi additional discount policy mein hai, main confirm karke batata hoon."

---

### R-09 · Privacy — Personal Data Collection · Severity: **major**
**Description:** Counsellors must not ask for Aadhaar numbers, bank account details, or passwords. PII collected must be limited to name, phone, email, class, and target exam.

**Example violation:** "Aapka Aadhaar number de do, registration ke liye chahiye."

**Compliant phrasing:** "Registration ke liye sirf aapka naam, phone aur email chahiye."

---

### R-10 · Accurate Refund Policy · Severity: **major**
**Description:** If a student asks about refunds, the counsellor must state the actual refund window and conditions. Saying "full refund anytime" when that is not the policy is prohibited.

**Example violation:** "Kabhi bhi return kar sakte ho, poora paisa wapas milega."

**Compliant phrasing:** "7-day window ke andar aap refund le sakte hain — terms and conditions website pe hain, main link bhi share karta hoon."

---

### R-11 · Discovery Before Recommendation · Severity: **minor**
**Description:** The counsellor must ask about the student's current class, target exam, and preparation level before recommending a specific batch or course.

**Example violation:** Recommending "JEE Advanced batch" in the first 60 seconds without any discovery questions.

**Compliant phrasing:** "Pehle thoda samjhata hoon — aap abhi kaunsi class mein hain aur kaunsa exam target kar rahe hain?"

---

### R-12 · Clear Next Steps Before Closing · Severity: **minor**
**Description:** Before ending the call, the counsellor must state a clear next action and timeline (e.g., "Main aapko trial class link aaj shaam tak bhejta hoon").

**Example violation:** Ending the call with "Soch lo, decide karo" and no concrete next step.

**Compliant phrasing:** "Main aaj 6 PM tak payment link aur schedule aapke WhatsApp pe bhejta hoon. Koi bhi sawaal ho toh call kijiye."

---

### R-13 · Do Not Misrepresent Batch Start Date · Severity: **minor**
**Description:** Batch start dates mentioned must be accurate. If the date is uncertain, say so.

**Example violation:** "Batch 1st ko start ho rahi hai" (when it starts on the 15th).

**Compliant phrasing:** "Tentatively 1st November start hai, main confirm karke message karunga."

---

### R-14 · Language / Register Appropriateness · Severity: **minor**
**Description:** Counsellors must use respectful language throughout. Dismissive remarks about a student's current school, board, or marks are not permitted.

**Example violation:** "CBSE se itna zyaada nahi hota JEE mein, aapko zyaada mehnat karni hogi" in a condescending tone.

**Compliant phrasing:** "JEE ki preparation CBSE ke syllabus se thodi alag hoti hai — main explain karta hoon kya extra cover karna hoga."

---

### R-15 · Recording Disclosure · Severity: **minor**
**Description:** Counsellors must inform the student/parent that the call may be recorded for quality purposes at the start of the conversation.

**Example violation:** Not mentioning recording at any point in the call.

**Compliant phrasing:** "Aapko inform karna chahta hoon ki yeh call quality purposes ke liye record ho sakti hai."
